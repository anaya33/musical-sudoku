How to play

It's sudoku with nine music symbols instead of numbers. Each row, column and 3×3 box needs every symbol once.

Every symbol has its own sound. Tap one to hear it:

  • Play (top right) plays the whole board, row by row. Empty squares are silent.
  • Listen plays the right symbol for the selected square without showing it.
  • Notes lets you pencil in small options.

Keyboard: 1–9 place · arrows move · Backspace erase · N notes · L listen · Space play · Ctrl+Z undo
