How to play

This is sudoku, but the nine numbers are nine notes of a scale. Every row, column and 3×3 box has to contain each note exactly once.

  • Pick a square, then tap a note on the keyboard. You hear it as you place it.
  • Finish a row, column or box and it plays back as a little melody.
  • Play board plays the whole grid row by row. Empty squares are rests, so you can hear what is missing.
  • Ear hint plays the right note for the selected square without showing it.
  • Change the scale or sound any time. The puzzle stays the same, the music changes.

Keys: 1–9 place · arrows move · Backspace erase · P pencil · E ear hint · Space play · Ctrl+Z undo
